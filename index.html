Kaggle locked my account, and I am sad

Posted on 2022-01-10

Sort of upset, or sad today.
I wrote some code and upload it to Kaggle, but all of sudden it says my account is locked.
Searched for the reason, it says it might because fraud detection algorithm detects some suspicion activity from the account. Interesting, I just use it as a normal user do.
Actually I really like this platform, I wrote an article in which I strongly argue that I don’t like traditional way of education, you learn something, you pass an exam, you get an certification, then you are an expert.
What I appreciate is, if you are a researcher, do you like this research, are you doing the research on daily basis.
And I think the platform like Kaggle, or Github, they are a platform like this, it can record how often you contribute to the world, and how good you are.
While, a cold lock can destroy everything.

It seems I should embrace more on a decentralization world. Sad.
