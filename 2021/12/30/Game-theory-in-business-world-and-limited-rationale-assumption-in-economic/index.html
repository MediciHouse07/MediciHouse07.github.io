Game theory in business world and limited rationale assumption in economic

Posted on 2021-12-30


One stupid question has been in my mind for quiet a long time about the application of machine learning on the subject as economy, social sciences, sales & marketing…
It is different comparing with apply the ML to physic experiment, for social sciences related subjects, I would think there would be some sort of “suspicion chain” effect when we apply the technique on the non-physical subject. I would introduce it with an example.
(not sure if “suspicion chain” were interpreted correctly, I quote it from a famous Chinese book “Three-body”)

For example:

Year1:
Company A, used their market data building a model, the data were collected from the past, the purpose is to predict the future sales volume, then they build production plan basing on the prediction, they arrange KPIs to the sales guys, they create market campaign and so on and so forth.

Year2:
With the help of the ML, Company A made a prefect business performance in Year 1, they decided to keep using ML in Year2.
But, Company B step in, they decided to use ML as well.

Things begin to be interesting since then,
Company B knows that Company A was using ML in Year1, and it was quiet successful, and also, Company A really value the result that ML model gives.
then Company B have a big idea, when they implementing ML model, not only they would consider the past market data, they will also consider what Company A would do.
remember, at this time Company A has no idea that Company B would targeting on their trustworthy model. (it is Company B’s top secret, don’t tell others)

then, things would become even interesting in Year3

Year3:
Company A knows that Company B were targeting their model, and basing on that, Company B setup the whole company strategy in Year 2.
So, besides the market data, Company A decides to include Company B’s model in their model.(short for 1st round guess)
But Company B is a new comer, new comer somehow is always with good ideas(kidding), at this time, not only they would consider company A’s model, they would start planning with 2 scenario.

Company A knows Company B’s model, and Company A would include Company B’s model into their consideration, as well as market data(1st round guess)
Company A doesn’t know, or doesn’t get the Company B’s model correctly, then Company A would still use market data as their input
Basing on above 2 scenario, company B build a model, obviously it is with more thinking, they did more work then silly A, then B win. (short for 2nd round guess)
…

Year n:
both Company A and Company B would think about their strategy, their model.
each of them would perform n round or n+m round or even infinite round of guesses. obviously the one with more computing power would win.

The chain is established then.

Today, I didn’t see people building model with that, they just use the historical data.
As if market would be the same, always as the past.
As if other companies would not change their strategy dramatically because other company has considered your idea into account.

Of course the physical world would not change their so called “strategy”.
