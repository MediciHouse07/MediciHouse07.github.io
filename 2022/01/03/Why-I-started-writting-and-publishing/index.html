Why I started writting and publishing

Posted on 2022-01-03


The medieval age(476-1453), also known as the dark age.

In some sense, dark means there was only a few of material available for people to read at that period, which means historian who study it have to spend lots of time on figuring out historical puzzle through extremely limited resources. And that is bad.
To be honest, at that period, for the ordinary being, it is hard for them to record things, because they didn’t have available tools for them to record. Actually it was even impossible for people to get a piece of paper, at that time, paper was really expensive. Unless you are as rich as Da Vinci, and have a good dad. (Because his father is a notary, a notary needs paper to work)
So, lacking of proper tools is one of the reason that make the medieval so “dark”.

But there is also another reason will contribute the “dark”, which is, common sense.
If a thing is deemed as a common sense, there is no willing for people to talk about that, not to mention to recording it, unless you want to prove that you are a boring person.
For example, for the people living in today, everyone knows what is “twitter”, actually there is no need for us to record it.
While if we didn’t record it, it would probably take one hundred years for the historian living in the future to understand that twitter doesn’t equal to twit, and another one hundred years to understand it doesn’t equal to Taylor Swift (maybe it will cost 10 years for a robot historian, I mean if we lost the battle with AI ).

So, no matter what you want to record, just record, it is valuable, for any sense. if above argument made any sense.
With this idea in my mind, I decide to write something down, actually I have been doing this for quite a while, just didn’t publish them, instead I saved them in my local desktop or a mobile HDD.
While recently I started to worry, what if I lost my computer, what if my mobile HDD is broken, and how do I know if I can live till tomorrow.
Therefore, I decide to record things and publish things, do it more actively.

Because it helps, in any sense. And it was because of people help people, human being became the strongest race in the earth (Will night elf agree it? kidding)

A minor trace of an living ever exists
A bard who is always writting
