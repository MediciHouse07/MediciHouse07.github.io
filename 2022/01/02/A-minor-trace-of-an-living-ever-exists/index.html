A minor trace of an living ever exists

Posted on 2022-01-02


I watched part of the animation version of Matrix again.
I cried again.

Today is the second holiday days of the new year, 2022.
And I felt anxious since the first day, anxious about living, money, health, nobody knows what their future would be, I mean person.
Words live, in condition that big company can protect their server well, but that thing is prone to happen.

When I was trying to write something, I felt peace, this is good, though it still require caffeine and music, and most importantly, nobody is decorating their house.
In these days, I have been accompanied by the voice of decorating, normally people would not decorate their house unless they own one, that’s a perfect thing, and it is a thing that will never belong to me.
I am a poor guy. Say sorry to the world, I didn’t bring fortune to you.

In the last paragraph I said when I was writing something, I felt peace, while, the neighbour is decorating their house, the voice is big enough to kill me, my peace disapeared.

As I said, today is the second day of the whole holiday, I spent some time to fix something today, 2/3 of the daylight of today. That’s bad, cause holiday is precious, I shouldn’t waste it.
But, who can contrl?
Is there anyone can control their life? 100% I am not one of them.


Hope I can be this bird, I mean the one who is peaceful.
