A bard who is always writting

Posted on 2022-01-04


As I said in this article Why-I-started-writting-and-publishing, no record is invaluable, but sometimes, you would lose lots of insights because that you want to produce a super perfect article.
Say, I come up an idea on today, but today I don’t have too much energy to work out a poetic article, then I give up, and say let me write it on someday. But insights, inspiration disappears, it appears here, today, doesn’t mean it will wait for your energy.

So, I think it is better to write diary, in the diary, no matter you had energy or not, you can write, you can structure it in a nonstructural way, nobody would blame you, at least yourself shouldn’t, because you were trying to save your inspiration that probably will disappear on tomorrow.

Morning, my wife and I went to the library(actually it is not a library, just a book store equipped with a coffee shop), we stayed there for 2+ hours. It was nice and peace.
At that time, I re-watched the economic of money and banking course, it was good and brilliant, every time I watch it, I will be inspired.
Then I started reading book, called “A great game”, finally I finished the 4th chapter.

In the afternoon, we had a lunch in the Mcdonald’s, together with the food bought from KFC.
We know that it is not that healthy, but, we had a lot of fun.

Then, back to home, in the afternoon, I reviewed linear algebra course, it is good, I really like that course, it is a good way of training your brain.
By the time I wrote this article, it was 15:17, it was not a night, so you might say if it was too early to conclude a day, I would say no, it is not too early. What I should do is to record thing no matter when, I should record it as long as I had a time. Because only in this way, I can catch all my ideas, or things that probably can inspire me in the future.

A few scattered thought I would record down below.

  • The supplier of truth should be a few, otherwise there would not be only a few things that are real around you.
  • Everything has its supplier and its buyer
  • ABCP: Asset backed commercial paper
  • Use a row times a matrix means you are calculating a new row with the combination of the rows in the matrix
  • I would like to write some R package as another way to repay the world
  • Where are the places that you want to build your career?

OK, that’s it, above concludes my today’s diary.
