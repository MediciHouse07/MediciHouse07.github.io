Are you happy today? Discounted happiness & Death

Posted on 2022-02-08

Are you happy today? How happy are you?
Have you ever thought that, the happiness level of you at a specific day is not solely determined by what you experienced at that day.


For example, one day, you are not very lucky, because you are late and miss an important meeting, but probably you are still so happy, because you won a lottery several days ago, and in the next week you will become a billionaire.
Another example, normally people would feel happy if they were on vacation, but at some precise day of the vacation people would feel bad, like, the last day of the vacation.
Even though you are still off at that day, you can still do whatever you want to do, but everyone knows you are unhappy.

See? It seems the happiness of a day, or a moment, has something to do with the future.
I can’t help to link this idea with an interesting concept commonly used in finance, which is time value of money, or discount.
For example:

If you were a 6 years old kid, and you had 10 USD in your pocket, if someone told you to lend it to them, and they will repay you 10 USD in one year, would you agree?
I guess you would not, why do I have to wait for 1 year to spend this money, I want to spend them now, sugar can’t wait.
But if this person said, they would repay you 11 USD in one year, what would you say in this time?
Maybe you would take the deal, maybe not, but you are swayed. If the term turns to be 100 USD in the next year I guess you would accept it totally, that means infinite sugar, who can say no to that?

In turn, still this case, what does the 11 USD in the next year worth in terms of today?
10 USD, right?

The concept of time value of money lies in the story.

Let’s back to the happiness case,
You are happy, you are unhappy, it is not a problem of today(one single day), it relates with all the things expecting to be happened in the future.

Take a 7 days vacation as an example:

Stage 1. Actually, you would start feeling happy in the 7 days before the vacation,
Gradually your happiness will be growing, at the eve of the first off day your happiness would hit the peak
Stage 2. Then you are entering the vacation, your inventory of vacation is beginning to decrease, at the beginning you can’t feel it, because you are so rich at this moment.
Stage 3. Until the day that you are in the middle of the vacation, you starts to worry.
Stage 4. Then the eve of the working day, your happiness begin to “free fall”

Limited time scope when discounting happiness
If you were a 10 years old child, you wouldn’t worry about death, probably.
It would only happen when you are not that young, this illustrates a difference comparing with the time value of money, because big firm can discount whatever period of loan to today, firm lives much longer than human being, but the fact is, the young doesn’t worry much of the death of themselves, just like you would not worry about your vacation at the first day off.
There is some sort of limitation on the discount period of your happiness.

Speaking of the death, how many happiness points it can be discounted to today? Because if it were a negative infinite happiness… luckily that we can only feel & discount that at a specific period of our life.
Actually, this is a question that is hard to answer, but at least I guess you would quote a negative figure to it, at least myself would.

I will write another article to discuss the discount of the death.
